Celestile

A turn-based 4X strategy game. Claim a cluster of space one tile at a time, out-think up to fourteen rival empires, and win by holding every planet or by finishing ahead of all of them.

Combat
Deterministic. The forecast above a fight is the result. No dice.
Two ways to win
Domination — hold every planet. Ascendancy — finish ahead inside 30 rounds.
Choices
8 factions, each a different plan. 8 unit classes. 20 technologies.
Plays
Single-player and offline. No account, no connection, no chat.
Ships in
Nine languages, with 47 achievements and a career record that persists.

Timber Frenzy

A base defense game. Pick a stage from camp, then hold a forest clearing through seven waves with your hero, your turret and the animal fighting beside you — and spend what you carry back.

Runs
100 stages, across ten Ages from a canvas camp to a space base.
Plays
Offline, with an anonymous cloud save. No account.
Out now
Free on iOS. Google Play to follow.
