Privacy Policy — Celestile on the web

Applies to the browser version of Celestile, including the versions playable on CrazyGames and Poki. Last updated 30 August 2026.

The short version

Frosty Robot Studio does not collect anything from you in this version of the game. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no server of ours involved. We do not receive your name, your email address, your location or any record of how you play.

What the game stores

Celestile saves your progress so you can come back to a game in progress. That includes your current match, your settings, your career record and achievements, your Alloy balance, and which factions and cosmetics you have unlocked.

This information stays with the site you are playing on. On CrazyGames it is kept using their save system, which means it can follow you between devices when you are signed in to CrazyGames. Elsewhere it is stored in your own browser and never leaves your device. Either way it is game progress and nothing else — it contains no personal information, and it is not sent to us.

Clearing your browser's site data removes the copy held in your browser. Progress kept by the site hosting the game is governed by that site's own privacy policy and can be managed through your account there.

Advertising

Celestile shows an occasional full-screen advert between matches, and offers optional adverts you can choose to watch in exchange for in-game Alloy. You never have to watch one to keep playing.

These adverts are served by the site hosting the game — CrazyGames or Poki — not by us. We do not run an advertising network, we do not build profiles, and we receive no information about you from the adverts. How that site selects adverts, and what choices you have about it, is covered by their privacy policy.

Purchases

There are no purchases in the web version of Celestile. Factions and cosmetics are earned with Alloy, which is earned by playing. No payment details are ever requested or handled.

Children

Celestile is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Other versions of Celestile

The App Store and Google Play versions, and the TikTok Mini Game version, are separate products with different data practices. Each has its own policy: App Store and Google Play · TikTok Mini Game.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.
